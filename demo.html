Etiology & Visible Symptoms

Early blight is a prevalent fungal disease caused by Alternaria solani. It primarily affects older foliar tissue, exhibiting dark, irregular necrotic spots characterized by distinctive concentric rings ("target board" pattern) surrounded by chlorotic yellow halos.

General Agronomic Suggestions

Pruning: Remove and safely dispose of affected bottom leaves to curb fungal spore transmission.
Targeted Spray: Apply bio-rational or copper-based preventive fungicides according to package directions.
Irrigation Hygiene: Transition to drip irrigation to keep foliage dry; avoid overhead watering.
