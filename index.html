Project Overview

Intelligent Plant Health, Explained Simply

The BIOSHIELD AI project demonstrates how an artificial intelligence system could inspect leaf photographs, recognize subtle visual pathology patterns, classify candidate diseases, and present clear, actionable plant-care suggestions.

Leaf Image Analysis

Examines high-resolution photographs of single leaves captured through mobile phones or webcams, focusing computational attention on textural leaf venation and discoloration.

Clear single-leaf focal framing
Standardized 224×224 tensor input

Disease Pattern Recognition

Utilizes deep Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) trained on plant pathology datasets to recognize distinct symptoms such as concentric rings, water-soaked spots, and chlorosis.

Multi-scale feature extraction
Transparent probability scoring

Plant Care Recommendations

Pairs diagnostic findings with educational horticultural context, covering biological pathogen causes, preventive cultural practices, and non-invasive remediation steps.

Pathogen biology explanations
Pruning & sanitation guidelines
