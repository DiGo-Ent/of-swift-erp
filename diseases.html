Pathology Library

Demonstrated Disease Categories

A botanical overview of the four benchmark conditions evaluated within the BIOSHIELD AI computer vision conceptual model.

Healthy Foliage

Healthy Leaf

Baseline Physiology

Serves as the indispensable negative baseline in neural network training, enabling convolutional filters to distinguish normal cellular textures from early necrotic onset.

Key Diagnostic Signs:
Rich, uniform chlorophyll coloration; intact cuticle layer; absence of wilting, chlorosis, or necrotic spotting.
Fungal Blight

Early Blight

Alternaria solani

A widespread fungal disease heavily impacting tomatoes and potatoes. It initiates on older lower leaves, forming dark lesions before moving up the canopy.

Key Diagnostic Signs:
Dark brown to black necrotic lesions with distinct concentric target rings, surrounded by yellow chlorotic halos.
Oomycete Decay

Late Blight

Phytophthora infestans

An aggressive, moisture-loving pathogen capable of decimating crop canopies within days. Historically responsible for the European potato blight.

Key Diagnostic Signs:
Irregular water-soaked olive-green to dark brown patches, often fringed with delicate white mycelium in high humidity.
Bacterial / Fungal

Leaf Spot

Septoria / Cercospora spp.

Characterized by countless discrete circular lesions caused by various fungal or bacterial agents that compromise the photosynthetic surface.

Key Diagnostic Signs:
Small circular spots (1-3mm) with ash-gray centers and dark borders, resulting in premature leaf yellowing and drop.
Pathology Comparison

Comparative Diagnostic Summary

Category Pathogen Type Lesion Morphology High-Risk Weather Primary Field Action
Healthy Leaf None (Normal Plant) Uniform leaf blade, no spots Optimal conditions Maintain nutrition & routine scouting
Early Blight Fungal (A. solani) Concentric rings, target spots Warm (24–29°C) & humid Prune bottom foliage, apply bio-fungicide
Late Blight Oomycete (P. infestans) Water-soaked irregular patches Cool (15–20°C) & wet Immediate removal, isolate affected row
Leaf Spot Fungal/Bacterial (Septoria) Small circular spots, gray center Prolonged leaf wetness Drip irrigation, increase airflow spacing

Note on Illustrated Categories: These four conditions represent standard benchmarking categories in computer vision datasets (such as PlantVillage). This showcase does not encompass all plant species or crop pathogens.
